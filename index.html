Our World

Three ideas, one table.

Everything at Berries starts from the same place — good ingredients, unrushed hands, and a room where you're glad to linger.

The Story

What began as a family table in Durg grew into a café built around the same idea — cook properly, host warmly, rush nothing.

Every recipe on our menu has been tested, argued over, and adjusted at that same table before it ever reached a guest. That's still how new dishes are approved today.

The Craft

Espresso pulled to order, seasonal produce, and a kitchen that treats a breakfast plate with the same care as dinner service.

Our baristas train on the same machine every guest is served from, and our chefs rebuild the seasonal menu around what's genuinely good that week — not a fixed rotation.

The Welcome

We keep the room small on purpose, so the people running it can actually remember your name and your usual order.

Ask us for a quiet corner, a birthday candle, or a dish that isn't on the menu — the answer, more often than not, is yes.
